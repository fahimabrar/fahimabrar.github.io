INNF 5.2(a)Innovative

"A genuine, original business plan that meets new or existing market needs and/or creates a competitive advantage."

What endorsers test: is there something proprietary, defensible and absent from the UK market? Did the founder build it, or only describe it?

INNF 5.2(b)Viable

"Realistic and achievable based on the applicant's available resources; the applicant has, or is actively developing, the skills, knowledge, experience and market awareness to run it."

What endorsers test: does the CV match the business? Is there enough money? Customer validation, MVP, early revenue?

INNF 5.2(c)Scalable

"Evidence of structured planning and of potential for job creation and growth into national and international markets."

What endorsers test: hiring plan, a revenue model that grows faster than cost, export potential, a credible route to 2 of the 7 settlement milestones.

Graduate pathway

From a UK master's to a founder visa

For an overseas graduate finishing a UK degree in a technical field. University ranking is irrelevant to every route below. What matters is the subject, being in the UK already, and the date the Graduate visa is applied for.

Assumes the Graduate visa is applied for in the month the course ends. Applications on or before 31 Dec 2026 get 24 months (36 for doctorates); from 1 Jan 2027, 18 months (36 for doctorates). A placement must be an integral, assessed part of the course and no more than 50% of its length at degree level. Decision point allows ~4 months for endorsement (4–6 weeks) plus an in-country decision (up to 8 weeks). Rules projected forward; they changed three times in 2026.

If the business idea does not pass the three tests

Since May 2026 there is no owner-operator fallback. The options are: take a sponsored role with someone else's company (Skilled Worker), build a portfolio toward Global Talent, or spend the Graduate visa turning the idea into one that does pass: customer evidence, a prototype, a patent search, a co-founder for any skills gap. Say this early; it saves the client an endorsement fee and a consultant's bill.
