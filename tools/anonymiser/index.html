What is downloaded, from where, and what is never sent
  • Downloaded once, then stored in this browser: the PDF reader and writer libraries; the OCR engine and English language data, fetched only the first time you open a scanned PDF; and, when the box above is ticked, the name-detection model and its runtime. Every one of these is served from this site, the same address you are on now. No third-party server is contacted. They are public, open-source components (pdf.js, pdf-lib, tesseract.js, Transformers.js and the bert-base-NER model), and the requests carry no information about you or your documents.
  • Never sent anywhere: your text, your PDFs, the detected items, your redactions, the exported files and the audit log. They exist only in this tab and in this browser's storage on this computer.
  • Check for yourself: press F12, open the Network tab, then paste text or upload a PDF. Once the components above are stored, you will see no requests at all. You can also switch the internet off and keep working.
  • Remove everything: clear this site's data in your browser settings. The app will simply download the components again next time.

How to use
1. Paste or type text, then click Identify Personal Info, or upload a PDF.
2. Coloured highlights show detected PII. Click any to redact it, or click Anonymise All to redact every item at once.
3. Click any other word to redact it manually.
4. Copy or download the anonymised text.
